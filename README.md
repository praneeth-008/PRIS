PRIS is a Python-based multi-asset market-risk engine that measures portfolio P&L, VaR and Expected Shortfall, attributes risk to positions and systematic factors, validates models through rolling out-of-sample backtesting, and evaluates portfolio vulnerability under historical and hypothetical stress scenarios.

## Current development scope

- Multi-asset portfolio and P&L engine
- Data-quality and return-validation controls
- Historical and parametric VaR/Expected Shortfall
- EWMA and GARCH-based conditional risk models
- Position, asset-class, and factor attribution
- Rolling VaR backtesting
- Kupiec and Christoffersen validation tests
- Historical and hypothetical stress testing

## Planned extensions

- Options valuation and Greeks
- Full versus delta-gamma revaluation
- Liquidity-adjusted VaR
- Yield-curve PCA
- Regime-sensitive models
- Reverse stress testing
- Interactive dashboard
